# SonderChess 1.0.0

The first release. Free game review for your Chess.com games, with Stockfish running on your phone: no sign-up, no subscription, no server.

## Game review
- Share a game from Chess.com (live, daily or bot), paste a game link, or any PGN.
- Accuracy for both players, an estimated game rating, the opening, the evaluation graph and a count of every move label.
- Every move labelled Brilliant, Great, Best, Excellent, Good, Book, Inaccuracy, Mistake, Miss or Blunder, with the coach explaining it, an arrow for the better move, and Next to jump to the moments that matter.
- Fast, Normal or Deep analysis with Stockfish 17.1 on the device.

## Your games
- Choose a Chess.com player to see the profile, ratings and full game history, with everyone's pictures.
- Stats: rating graphs and records per time control, puzzle ratings, and your brilliant moves.
- Works offline: reviews and your games stay on the phone and update when you're back online.

## Play
- Bots from Pebble (400) to Titan (2500), plus SR, Mirror (plays at your rating) and Custom (any rating from 400 to 3000).
- Stockfish at full strength.
- A custom board: move both sides, set up any position, and switch the engine on for its evaluation and best lines.
- Hints, undo, and a full review when the game ends.

## Puzzles
- 46,345 puzzles from the Lichess database that work offline, picked at your level, with your own puzzle rating and streak.
- Chess.com's daily puzzle.

## And
- 20 boards, 7 piece sets, backgrounds or your own photo, 5 sound sets with a sound of its own for brilliant moves.
- Swipe along the bottom bar to move between Home, Puzzles, Play, Review and More.
